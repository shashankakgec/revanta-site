Does submitting the form confirm my appointment?+

No. It sends an appointment request. The clinic needs to contact you to confirm the date and time.

What if I'm nervous about dental treatment?+

Let the clinic know when they contact you. You can ask questions and discuss comfort needs before treatment.

Can I ask about a service not listed here?+

Yes. Choose “Other / not sure yet” and the clinic can discuss your request with you.
